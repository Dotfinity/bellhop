# ST-025 — Skip waiting occurrences when a recurring job stops, and record each outcome

**Milestone:** M1 · **Epic:** m1-core-preview · **Priority:** must · **Depends on:** ST-024, ST-018

## Context

Finishes the occurrence lifecycle. When a recurring job is paused, orphaned or removed, its waiting occurrences are dropped, so nothing starts for a job that is no longer active (REQ-237). Each occurrence's outcome becomes the recurring job's last outcome (REQ-231).

Builds on ST-024 (occurrences under every overlap policy, REQ-239's meaning of unfinished, and the supersede of REQ-240) and ST-018 (cancellation, whose Cancelled state is one of REQ-231's outcomes). It keeps INV-204 holding: ST-024's property test runs again with REQ-237's skips added. REQ-237 extends writes from earlier stories: ST-022's orphaning and Delete removal (REQ-205, REQ-207), and ST-023's pause and removal (REQ-214, REQ-213). ST-026 builds on it: REQ-242's record says whether each occurrence a trigger created started, waits or was skipped. So do ST-034 (the recurring page shows the last outcome, dashboard REQ-310) and ST-072 (the occurrence history, dashboard REQ-329).

Lives in the Bellhop core package (TECH-112). The last outcome is read through IRecurringJobManager's last-outcome method (TECH-21, ST-023). What the last outcome holds is open question 1.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 · REQ-237 · must

> When a recurring job is paused, orphaned or removed while it has waiting occurrences, the system shall record each waiting occurrence as skipped with reason Paused, Orphaned or Removed.
>
> *Transition:* Occurrence: Waiting → Skipped · *Actor:* system
>
> *Notes:* refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-8 (decided 2026-11-06, recommended option): pausing, orphaning and removing drop the waiting occurrences, so nothing starts for a job that is no longer active.

- Given a Queue(2) job with two waiting occurrences, when an operator pauses it (REQ-214), then both are recorded as Skipped with reason Paused. Neither starts later, not even after a resume (REQ-215).
- Given the same job is orphaned by the scheduler (REQ-205), the reason is Orphaned. Given it is removed by the operator (REQ-213) or by orphanPolicy Delete (REQ-207), the reason is Removed.
- Given a Started occurrence of that job whose job is processing, then the pause, orphaning or removal leaves the occurrence and its job unchanged. Only waiting occurrences are skipped.
- Concurrency: given a pause and REQ-224's start of the same waiting occurrence at once, then the occurrence ends either Started with one job, if the start landed first, or Skipped with reason Paused with no job. Never both (recurring A-30: the skip applies to the occurrences still waiting at the write).
- Given the pause, orphaning or removal's own compare-and-set loses (TECH-45), then no occurrence is skipped by that write.

### AC-2 · REQ-231 · must

> When an occurrence's job succeeds, is dead-lettered or is cancelled, or an occurrence is skipped, the system shall record that outcome as the recurring job's last outcome.

- Given an occurrence whose job succeeds, then IRecurringJobManager's last outcome for the recurring job is that success. The same holds for a job that is dead-lettered, and for one cancelled for any reason (an operator's cancel, CancelPrevious, Superseded).
- Given an occurrence that is skipped (under Skip, with reason QueueFull, or with reason Paused, Orphaned or Removed), then the last outcome is that skip.
- Given an occurrence's job fails and waits for its retry, becomes Unroutable, or is still processing, then the last outcome is not changed: none of these is a listed outcome.
- Given a later outcome, then it replaces the earlier one (REQ-242's rationale: the last outcome is overwritten by the next one).
- Property: over random sequences of occurrences and job outcomes with one occurrence running at a time (Skip), the last outcome always equals the most recent listed outcome.
- With outcomes arriving out of order under Allow or CancelPrevious, which one is "last" is open question 1.
