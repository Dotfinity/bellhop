# ST-011 — Retry failing jobs and dead-letter them

**Milestone:** M1 · **Epic:** m1-core-preview · **Priority:** must · **Depends on:** ST-008, ST-010

## Context

Makes a failing job try again and then give up visibly. A handler exception marks the job failed with retryAt from an exponential backoff with jitter (REQ-100, REQ-129), and the job is enqueued again once retryAt passes by the database clock (REQ-130). When attempts run out, the retry policy declines, or the handler throws PermanentJobFailureException, the job is dead-lettered (REQ-131, SCN-101). Every failed attempt keeps the exception details and the node that ran it (INV-117). The retry policy is a replaceable IRetryPolicy (TECH-26).

Builds on ST-008: the attempt number counted at claim (REQ-123) and the transition history (INV-116), which must keep holding and which records the new transitions. It also builds on ST-007's promotion duty, which REQ-130 extends to failed jobs and which must stay exactly-once (INV-115), and the database clock (INV-105). Settings per job type and per enqueue use [Retry] (TECH-17, ST-002), fluent configuration (INV-123) and EnqueueOptions' retry policy override (TECH-20, ST-007); ST-002's INV-123 must keep holding for MaxAttempts and the backoff. It builds on ST-010 for IJobEvents: its failed and dead-lettered transitions raise OnFailed and OnDeadLettered, so ST-010's REQ-169 must keep holding. Promotion of failed jobs is a worker duty, so ST-007's hosting REQ-40 must keep holding. REQ-129's "its cancellation was not requested" and its shutdown clause are built against what exists: no job has a cancellation request before ST-018 and no token is cancelled for shutdown before ST-017; both keep REQ-129 holding, and so does ST-014 for timeouts. Built on by ST-013 (a lease that expires at the last attempt dead-letters, REQ-133), ST-014 (a timeout is an ordinary failed attempt), ST-015 (failures and dead letters in the health thresholds, hosting REQ-34) and ST-020 (requeuing dead letters).

Lives in the core package (TECH-112): IRetryPolicy and PermanentJobFailureException (TECH-26), resolved from DI (TECH-13), and the engine's handling of a handler's outcome. The members of IRetryPolicy and the names of the settings for MaxAttempts and the backoff are not in the specs (open question 1).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 · REQ-100 · must

> The system shall retry a failing job up to MaxAttempts = 5 attempts in total by default, with exponential backoff and jitter of roughly 10 s, 1 min, 5 min and 30 min between attempts, configurable globally, per job type and per enqueue.
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06: per-enqueue options beat the job type's settings, which beat the global default (see INV-123 for attribute versus fluent).

- Given nothing is configured, when a job's handler throws on every attempt, then the job runs 5 attempts in total and is then dead-lettered (REQ-131), and the waits between its attempts, read from retryAt against each failure's time, are roughly 10 s, 1 min, 5 min and 30 min.
- Given many jobs failing at the same moment with the defaults, then their retryAt values are spread by the jitter rather than equal.
- Given MaxAttempts is set to 3 globally, then an always-failing job is dead-lettered after its third attempt.
- Given the job type sets MaxAttempts by attribute ([Retry], TECH-17) and fluently, then the fluent value is used (INV-123); given a per-enqueue value too, then the per-enqueue value is used, then the job type's, then the global default (REQ-100's notes).
- Given a backoff configured globally, per job type or per enqueue, then retryAt follows it with the same precedence.
- How far "roughly" may stray, and the delay after the fourth wait, are open question 2.

### AC-2 · REQ-129 · must

> While a job is processing and its cancellation was not requested, when its handler throws an exception, if the exception is not an OperationCanceledException thrown after the job's token was cancelled for shutdown, and the attempt number is below maxAttempts and the retry policy allows another attempt, then the system shall mark the job failed and set retryAt from the backoff.
>
> *Transition:* Job: Processing → Failed · *Actor:* Handler
>
> *Notes:* Resolved after refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-7: a job whose cancellation was requested is marked cancelled instead (REQ-190). F-8: an OperationCanceledException after the shutdown signal is REQ-140, not a failed attempt.

- Given a processing job at attempt 1 of 5 whose handler throws an ordinary exception, when the attempt ends, then the job is Failed with retryAt set from the backoff (REQ-100), its attempt number stays 1, and the transition is recorded with its time, node and reason (INV-116).
- Given the handler throws an OperationCanceledException although no token of the job was cancelled for shutdown, for example a TaskCanceledException from its own HTTP call, then it is an ordinary failure: Failed while attempts remain.
- Given the attempt number has reached maxAttempts, the retry policy declines, or the exception is a PermanentJobFailureException, then this rule does not apply; REQ-131 dead-letters the job.
- The cancellation and shutdown clauses are closed by ST-018 and ST-017 (see Context).

### AC-3 · REQ-130 · must

> While a job is failed, when its retryAt has passed according to the database clock, the system shall move the job to enqueued.
>
> *Transition:* Job: Failed → Enqueued · *Actor:* system

- Given a failed job whose retryAt has passed by the database clock, when a worker node's promotion runs, then the job is Enqueued with its attempt number unchanged, and its next claim makes it the next attempt (REQ-123).
- Given retryAt has not passed by the database clock, then the job stays Failed, also on a node whose own clock is already past retryAt (INV-105).
- Concurrency: given several worker nodes promoting at once, then the job is moved once, and its history holds one transition from Failed to Enqueued for that retry (INV-115).
- Given only nodes without the worker run, then the job stays Failed until a worker node runs (hosting REQ-40).

### AC-4 · REQ-131 · must

> While a job is processing, when its handler throws an exception, if the exception is not an OperationCanceledException thrown after the job's token was cancelled for shutdown, and the attempt number has reached maxAttempts, the retry policy declines another attempt, or the exception is a PermanentJobFailureException, then the system shall move the job to dead-lettered.
>
> *Transition:* Job: Processing → DeadLettered · *Actor:* Handler
>
> *Notes:* Same event as REQ-129; the guards are complementary, so exactly one of the two applies. PermanentJobFailureException skips retries (RET-2). Resolved after refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-8: an OperationCanceledException after the shutdown signal is REQ-140, not a failure. Run 2 F-1 (recommended option): this applies whether or not cancellation was requested; REQ-190 takes only the failures that would have been retried.

- Given a job at attempt 5 of 5 whose handler throws, then the job is DeadLettered and is never claimed again.
- Given attempt 1 of 5 and an IRetryPolicy that declines another attempt, then the job is DeadLettered.
- Given attempt 1 of 5 and a PermanentJobFailureException, then the job is DeadLettered with no retryAt and no further attempt.
- Property: over random exceptions, attempt numbers and policy answers, exactly one of REQ-129 and REQ-131 decides each throw (REQ-131's notes), and the job ends Failed or DeadLettered accordingly.
- Whether a cancellation was requested does not change this rule (REQ-131's notes); ST-018 builds the request.

### AC-5 · INV-117 · must

> When an attempt fails, the system shall store the exception type, message and stack trace and the node that ran the attempt.

- Given an attempt whose handler throws, then the stored attempt has the exception's type, message and stack trace and the node that ran it, whether the job became Failed or DeadLettered.
- Given several failed attempts of one job on different nodes, then each attempt keeps its own details and node, and no later attempt overwrites an earlier one (SCN-101).
- Property: over random runs on two worker nodes, every failed attempt in a job's history has all four fields, and its node is the one that held the claim for that attempt.

### AC-6 · SCN-101 · scenario

> **Retries until dead-lettered**
>
> *Given:* A job whose handler always throws; maxAttempts = 2.
>
> 1. REQ-120
> 2. REQ-123 — attempt 1
> 3. REQ-129 — throws; retry scheduled
> 4. REQ-130 — retryAt passes
> 5. REQ-123 — attempt 2
> 6. REQ-131 — throws; attempts exhausted
>
> *Then:* The job is DeadLettered with two stored attempts, each with exception details and node.

### AC-7 · TECH-26 · must

> **Retry policy contract** — The core package shall define a replaceable IRetryPolicy resolved from DI and a PermanentJobFailureException that skips retries.

- Inspection: the core package defines a public IRetryPolicy and a public PermanentJobFailureException.
- Test: an IRetryPolicy registered in DI replaces the default; one that always declines dead-letters a job at its first failure, and one that always allows retries it until MaxAttempts.
- Test: with no IRetryPolicy registered, failures follow REQ-100's defaults.
