# ST-072 — Build and review recurring schedules in the dashboard

**Milestone:** M2 · **Epic:** m2-1-0 · **Priority:** must · **Depends on:** ST-047, ST-034, ST-068, ST-055, ST-056, ST-062

## Context

Lets an operator check a schedule before saving it and review it afterwards: while a recurring schedule is built or edited in the dashboard, the next five occurrences are previewed in the chosen time zone (REQ-328), and each recurring job shows its last 50 occurrences with outcome and duration, and its misfire, overlap and validity-window settings (REQ-329).

Builds on ST-034 (the recurring jobs page with its edit action, REQ-310, which this story keeps holding) and ST-047 (the validity window, INV-202), on top of ST-003 (schedule syntax, IANA zones and daylight-saving rules: TECH-23, TECH-24, TECH-25, recurring REQ-228 and REQ-229), ST-023 (next N occurrences, REQ-230, and the edit rules REQ-209, REQ-211 and REQ-212), ST-024 to ST-026 (occurrences, the overlap and misfire policies and the last outcome), ST-068 (Make recurring builds a schedule in the dashboard, so the preview applies there too; and INV-309, which this story keeps holding: the builder and its edit work on a node that only maps the dashboard) and ST-055, ST-056 and ST-062 (the SQL Server, SQLite and Redis stores, on which the history query is implemented). It keeps TECH-105 holding, with conformance tests for any store operation it adds. Built on by ST-075 (NFR-16 for these queries).

Lives in Bellhop.Dashboard (TECH-88) and its JSON API (TECH-92). The preview uses the same schedule evaluation as IRecurringJobManager's next N occurrences (TECH-21, REQ-230). The history is read from the store; where the store contract has no query for a recurring job's latest occurrences with their jobs' outcome and duration, it grows on every store that exists by now: in-memory, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, SQLite and Redis (TECH-31).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 · REQ-328 · must

> When an operator builds or edits a recurring schedule in the dashboard, the system shall preview the next five occurrences in the chosen time zone.

- Given an operator editing a recurring job's schedule, or building one with Make recurring (REQ-322), with a cron expression and the time zone Europe/Berlin, then the preview shows the next five occurrences after now, expressed in Europe/Berlin, equal to the first five that REQ-230 computes for that schedule and zone.
- Given the operator changes the expression or the zone, then the preview follows what is in the form, before anything is saved.
- Given a schedule whose next five occurrences cross a daylight-saving change in the chosen zone, then the previewed instants follow recurring REQ-228 (a skipped local time runs once at the next valid instant) and REQ-229 (a repeated one runs once, at its first instance).
- Given time mode Utc, then the preview is in UTC (REQ-200).
- Given an invalid expression, or time mode Local without a valid IANA time zone, then no occurrence is previewed and saving is rejected with an error that says what is wrong (REQ-209).
- Given a schedule with fewer than five occurrences left, then the preview shows those it has; it never shows more than five.
- Which clock "now" is read from, and whether the validity window trims the preview, are open question 1.

### AC-2 · REQ-329 · must

> The system shall show each recurring job's last 50 occurrences with outcome and duration, and its misfire, overlap and validity-window settings.

- Given a recurring job with 60 occurrences, when a viewer opens it, then the 50 most recent are shown, each with its outcome and duration, and the 10 older ones are not; given a job with 7 occurrences, all 7 are shown.
- Given occurrences in every state, then each shows its outcome: started with its job's result (succeeded, dead-lettered or cancelled), skipped with its reason (an overlap skip, QueueFull, Paused, Orphaned or Removed: REQ-222, REQ-235, REQ-237), or waiting (REQ-223).
- Given an occurrence whose job has finished, then its duration is shown.
- Given a job, then its misfire policy (RunOnce, Skip, or RunAll with its maximum), its overlap policy (Skip, Queue with its maximum, Allow or CancelPrevious) and its validity window (ActiveFrom and ActiveUntil, INV-202) are shown; a job that sets no policy shows RunOnce and Skip (REQ-201), and a job without a validity window shows none.
- Given two recurring jobs, then a job's list never shows the other's occurrences.
- What duration measures, what is shown for an occurrence without a finished job, and how long occurrences are kept are open questions 2 and 3.

## Open questions

1. **Decide in delivery** · REQ-328: is "now" for the preview the server's clock or the browser's, and does the preview leave out occurrences outside the job's validity window, which the scheduler never creates on schedule (INV-202)? REQ-230 computes from the schedule and its time zone alone.
2. **Decide in delivery** · REQ-329: what does "duration" measure (the last attempt's run, or the time from the first start to the end across retries), and what is shown for an occurrence that was skipped, is waiting or whose job has not finished?
3. **Decide in delivery** · REQ-329: how long are occurrences kept? A daily job's last 50 occurrences span 50 days, longer than RetentionSucceeded (7 days, jobs REQ-104), and no item says whether an occurrence outlives its job or what the page shows once retention has removed the job (jobs REQ-145). REQ-242 keeps missed-instant records as long as dead letters, but says nothing of occurrences.
