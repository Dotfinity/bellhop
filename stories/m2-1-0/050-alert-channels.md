# ST-050 — Send alerts through webhook, Slack, Teams and ILogger channels

**Milestone:** M2 · **Epic:** m2-1-0 · **Priority:** must · **Depends on:** ST-049, ST-048

## Context

Sends deliveries. It adds the channels, a generic JSON webhook with a message template (TECH-115), Slack and Microsoft Teams incoming-webhook presets (TECH-116, TECH-117) and an ILogger channel that logs at Critical (TECH-118), and the delivery lifecycle: Sent when the channel accepts (REQ-407), retried with backoff while attempts remain (REQ-408, REQ-412), and Failed, with an error log and a metric, when they run out (REQ-415). It also adds 'send test' to the Notifications page (REQ-409), which is the first way a delivery is created, outside the cooldowns.

Builds on ST-049 (AddAlerts(), the notification lease and its lease-checked delivery write, and the Notifications page), and keeps INV-411 holding as the first story that creates and sends deliveries. Builds on ST-048 and keeps its TECH-113 holding for webhook channels, which send through named HttpClient registrations, and INV-401 to INV-404 for every webhook, INV-404 covering credentials in a channel's configuration template. ST-051 builds rule deliveries and their cooldowns on this lifecycle (REQ-433 acts on REQ-415's Failed), ST-052 the remaining triggers, and ST-079 adds the SMTP channel (TECH-119).

The channels are part of the notification engine in the Bellhop core package (TECH-112), and the webhook channels send through named HttpClient registrations (TECH-113). The failure metric goes on the Bellhop meter (TECH-98). 'Send test' is on the Notifications page in Bellhop.Dashboard (TECH-88). A delivery's state and attempts are kept in the store, which INV-411's write updates, so they are implemented on the in-memory and PostgreSQL stores (TECH-31). REQ-409's "outside its cooldown" is built against what exists: cooldowns arrive in ST-051, and that criterion is closed there. A channel's configuration, the template syntax and the metric's name are not in the specs (open questions 6 and 8).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 · REQ-412 · must

> The system shall attempt each notification delivery at most NotificationMaxAttempts times, 5 by default, with backoff from about 10 seconds to 10 minutes between attempts (NotificationMaxAttempts).
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.

- Given NotificationMaxAttempts is not configured and a channel that rejects every message, then the delivery is attempted 5 times in total, the first included (integrations A-8), is then Failed (REQ-415), and is never attempted a sixth time.
- Given NotificationMaxAttempts is configured as 3, then the delivery is attempted 3 times and then Failed.
- Given the defaults and a fake TimeProvider, then the first retry comes about 10 seconds after the first attempt, the gaps between attempts never shrink, the last gap is about 10 minutes, and the five attempts span about 20 minutes (INV-410 notes).
- Given a delivery that a1 attempted before the lease moved to a2, then a2 continues with the attempts already counted, so the delivery still gets at most NotificationMaxAttempts attempts.
- The exact schedule and the tolerance of "about" are open question 5.

### AC-2 · REQ-407 · must

> While a delivery is pending, when its channel accepts the message, the system shall mark the delivery sent.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: Pending → Sent · *Actor:* system
>
> *Notes:* Channels: generic JSON webhook with a message template, Slack and Teams incoming-webhook presets, ILogger critical, and SMTP email (TECH-115 to TECH-119).

- Given a pending delivery on each channel in turn (webhook, Slack preset, Teams preset, ILogger), when the channel accepts the message, then the delivery is Sent and that attempt is counted.
- Given a Sent delivery, then it is never attempted again.
- Given a node that no longer holds the notification lease, then it cannot mark a delivery Sent (INV-411).
- What "accepts" means for each channel is open question 4.

### AC-3 · REQ-408 · must

> While a delivery is pending, if its channel rejects the message or cannot be reached, and fewer than NotificationMaxAttempts attempts have been made, then the system shall retry the delivery with backoff.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: Pending → Pending · *Actor:* system
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06: 5 attempts in total with backoff from about 10 s to 10 min, then Failed (REQ-415). Same event as REQ-415 with the complementary guard.

- Given a pending delivery whose webhook answers with a status it does not accept, refuses the connection, does not resolve or does not answer in time, and fewer than NotificationMaxAttempts attempts made counting this one (integrations A-8), then the delivery stays Pending and is attempted again after REQ-412's backoff, not before.
- Given the channel accepts on a later attempt, then the delivery is Sent (REQ-407) and no attempt follows.
- Given the rejected attempt is the NotificationMaxAttempts-th, then the delivery is not retried: REQ-415 applies.

### AC-4 · REQ-409 · must

> When an operator presses 'send test' on a rule on the Notifications page, the system shall create a test delivery for that rule, outside its cooldown.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: (new) → Pending · *Actor:* Operator
>
> *Notes:* Reading confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-06: a test delivery bypasses the cooldown, otherwise a test right after a real alert would be suppressed.

- Given an operator on the Notifications page, when they press 'send test' on rule R, then a test delivery for R is created Pending and sent through R's channel by the node that holds the notification lease (INV-411 notes), also when the page is served by a node without the alerts registration.
- Given R has just had a real delivery created Pending, which opens its cooldowns (INV-409, ST-051), when 'send test' is pressed, then the test delivery is still created Pending, not Suppressed; it neither opens nor extends a cooldown and does not count towards MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown, because INV-409, INV-410 and REQ-413 count non-test deliveries only.
- Given a test delivery that is rejected NotificationMaxAttempts times, then it is Failed and logged like any delivery (REQ-415).
- The permission 'send test' needs, and a test requested while no alerts node runs, are open questions 2 and 1.

### AC-5 · REQ-415 · must

> While a delivery is pending, if its channel rejects the message or cannot be reached, and NotificationMaxAttempts attempts have been made, then the system shall mark the delivery failed, log an error that names the rule and the channel, and count it in a metric.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: Pending → Failed · *Actor:* system
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06. Failed deliveries are listed on the Notifications page. Same event as REQ-408.

- Given a pending delivery with NotificationMaxAttempts − 1 attempts made, when its channel rejects it or cannot be reached, then it is Failed, one error is logged that names its rule and its channel, and the failure count on the Bellhop meter (TECH-98) goes up by one.
- Given fewer attempts made, then none of the three happens (REQ-408).
- Given a Failed delivery, then it is listed on the Notifications page (notes) and never attempted again.
- Given a webhook channel, then the logged error holds no secret from the channel's configuration (NFR-41); whether the webhook URL is one is open question 8.

### AC-6 · TECH-115 · must

> **Webhook notification channel** — The notification engine shall deliver to a generic JSON webhook with a message template.

- Test: given a webhook channel with a URL whose host is on the allowlist and a message template, when a delivery is sent, then the server receives one request whose body is JSON rendered from the template for that delivery, sent through a named HttpClient registration (TECH-113).
- Given a webhook whose host is off the allowlist, or covered only by a wildcard entry while it resolves to a private-network or loopback address, then no request is sent (INV-401, INV-402); a redirect is followed only as INV-403 allows. What the delivery becomes then is open question 3.
- The template's syntax is open question 6.

### AC-7 · TECH-116 · must
