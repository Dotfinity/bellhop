# ST-051 — Alert on dead letters at most once per cooldown, with digests

**Milestone:** M2 · **Epic:** m2-1-0 · **Priority:** must · **Depends on:** ST-050

## Context

Makes alerts quiet enough to keep: a rule sends at most one delivery per subject within a subject cooldown (INV-400) and at most MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown across subjects within a rule cooldown (INV-407, REQ-413), with one digest of what the cap suppressed when the rule cooldown ends (REQ-414). Both limits are decided when a delivery is created (INV-409), both cooldowns stay open while a delivery created in them is still being retried (INV-410), and a failed delivery closes its subject cooldown, so a channel outage does not silence the subject (REQ-433). The dead-letter rule (REQ-400) is the first trigger, and the one these tests drive.

Builds on ST-050 (the delivery lifecycle: Sent, retries and Failed, which REQ-433 reacts to; test deliveries, which never count) and through it on ST-049 (only the lease holder creates deliveries, SCN-405) and ST-011 (dead-lettering, jobs REQ-131). It keeps INV-411 holding for the rule deliveries it creates, and REQ-409 holding: a test delivery stays outside the cooldowns this story opens, the criterion ST-050 left to be closed here. ST-052 adds the other triggers on the same decision rule and keeps INV-409 holding. ST-052 also owns INV-412 and INV-413, which decide how REQ-400's dead-letters are found in the store once per event across lease handovers and where a new rule starts; until then REQ-400 fires in process on a combined worker and alerts node that holds the lease and sees its own dead-letters, and this story's handover tests drive INV-409's decision rule directly. ST-052 replaces that path, takes the alerts-only integration test, and must keep REQ-400, SCN-400 and SCN-405 green.

Part of the notification engine in the Bellhop core package (TECH-112). INV-400 and INV-407 must hold across a lease handover, so which deliveries are Pending and which cooldowns are open lives where every holder sees it: in the store, implemented on the in-memory and PostgreSQL stores (TECH-31). Delivery creation is serialised by INV-411's lease-checked write (integrations A-9), and cooldown lengths are measured with TimeProvider (TECH-12). Where a rule's cooldown and MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown are set is not in the specs (open question 2).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 · REQ-413 · must

> The system shall send at most MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown non-test deliveries per rule within its cooldown, 10 by default (MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown).
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.

- Given MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown is not configured, when one rule fires for 15 different subjects within one rule cooldown, then 10 deliveries are created Pending and 5 Suppressed, and no more than 10 are sent in that cooldown.
- Given MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown is configured as 3, then 3 are created Pending and 12 Suppressed.
- Given test deliveries for the same rule in that cooldown (REQ-409), then they are not counted, and all of them are sent.
- Given the previous rule cooldown's digest is sent within this one, then it is not counted, because a digest is never counted (INV-407, integrations A-5).

### AC-2 · REQ-400 · must

> Where a rule watches dead-lettering, when a job is dead-lettered, the system shall create a delivery for that rule with the job as subject.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: (new) → Pending | Suppressed · *Actor:* system

- Given a rule that watches dead-lettering, when a job is dead-lettered (jobs REQ-131, REQ-133 or REQ-186, or REQ-434) on a combined worker and alerts node that holds the lease, then one delivery for that rule with that job as subject is created, Pending or Suppressed as INV-409 decides.
- Given a job whose attempt fails with attempts left (Failed, jobs REQ-129), then this rule creates no delivery.
- Given a rule that watches something other than dead-lettering, then a dead-letter creates no delivery for it.
- Given two rules that watch dead-lettering, then each gets its own delivery for the same dead-letter.
- Given two combined nodes, when a2, which does not hold the lease, tries to create the delivery for a dead-letter, then its write fails and only the lease holder creates deliveries (SCN-405, INV-411).
- What the subject is when two jobs share a name, and whether a rule can filter by job name, is open question 1. A dead-letter made on another node, and exactly one delivery per dead-letter across handovers, are INV-412 (ST-052).

### AC-3 · REQ-414 · must

> When a rule cooldown ends and deliveries for its rule were suppressed by the per-rule cap during that cooldown, the system shall create one digest delivery for the rule that says how many were suppressed and for which subjects.
>
> *Transition:* Delivery: (new) → Pending · *Actor:* system
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06: a flood produces up to 10 messages plus one digest, never silence.

- Given the cap suppressed deliveries for subjects s11 to s15 during a rule cooldown, when that cooldown ends, then one digest delivery for the rule is created Pending that says 5 deliveries were suppressed and names s11 to s15, and it is sent through the rule's channel like any delivery (REQ-407, REQ-408, REQ-415).
- Given a rule cooldown in which nothing was suppressed, or only duplicates within a subject cooldown, then no digest is created when it ends.
- Given a delivery suppressed while its subject cooldown was open and the cap was reached, then it counts as a duplicate and is not in the digest (INV-409 notes).
- Given a delivery created in the cooldown is still Pending when the cooldown's length has passed, then the digest is created only when the cooldown ends after it (INV-410).
- Given a flood of 50 subjects within one rule cooldown and the defaults, then 10 deliveries and one digest naming the other 40 subjects are sent, never nothing (notes).
- The digest is never suppressed and never counted, since INV-409 and INV-407 cover non-digest deliveries only.

### AC-4 · INV-400 · must

> The system shall never send more than one non-test delivery for the same rule and subject within one subject cooldown; later ones within the subject cooldown are recorded as suppressed.
>
> *Notes:* The source says deliveries are 'rate-limited and deduplicated per rule and subject within a cooldown'. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06: there is also a cap per rule across subjects (INV-407) with a digest at the end of the rule cooldown (REQ-414). refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-1, F-2, F-4: enforced by deciding Pending or Suppressed at creation (INV-409) and keeping a cooldown open while a delivery created in it is still pending (INV-410); the model showed that combination makes INV-400 and INV-407 hold. A failed delivery closes its subject cooldown (REQ-433, F-3).

- Property, over random sequences of firings for random subjects, channel outcomes (accepts, rejects, unreachable), test deliveries and clock advances with a fake TimeProvider: for each rule and subject, at most one non-test delivery is Sent within one subject cooldown, and every later one within it is stored as Suppressed (IntegrationsNotifications.tla's Inv_OneSendPerSubjectCooldown), run with the model's bounds and with the defaults.
- Concurrency, on the in-memory and PostgreSQL stores: two firings for the same rule and subject at the same moment give one Pending and one Suppressed delivery.
- Concurrency: the lease moves from a1 to a2 inside an open subject cooldown; a delivery for that subject that a2 creates through INV-409's decision rule, called directly, is Suppressed.
- SCN-400 is the scenario of this rule.

### AC-5 · INV-407 · must

> The system shall never send more than MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown non-test, non-digest deliveries for the same rule within one rule cooldown, whatever their subjects; later ones within the rule cooldown are recorded as suppressed.
>
> *Notes:* Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06. refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-4: the rule cooldown is its own period of the rule's cooldown length, opened by INV-409.

- Property, the same generator with many subjects per rule: within one rule cooldown at most MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown non-test, non-digest deliveries are Sent, whatever their subjects, and the rest are stored as Suppressed (Inv_RuleCap), also when retries delay sends past the cooldown's length (INV-410).
- Given the subject cooldowns of different subjects opened at different moments, then the cap is counted per rule cooldown, the rule's own period of the rule's cooldown length (notes), not per subject cooldown.
- Concurrency: a lease handover inside an open rule cooldown does not reset the count, with deliveries created through INV-409's decision rule directly.

### AC-6 · INV-409 · must

> When a non-test, non-digest delivery is created for a rule and subject, the system shall create it as suppressed if a subject cooldown for that rule and subject is open, or if MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown non-test, non-digest deliveries for the rule were created as pending within its open rule cooldown; otherwise create it as pending and open, for the rule's cooldown length, the subject cooldown and, if none is open, the rule cooldown.
>
> *Notes:* refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-1 option 1 (decide at creation, counting deliveries created as pending) and F-4 option 1 (two periods of the rule's cooldown length: one per rule and subject, one per rule). This is the decision rule for the to: [Pending, Suppressed] of REQ-400 to REQ-406. Counting sent deliveries instead fails: two deliveries created before either is sent both see nothing sent. When both the subject cooldown and the cap apply, the delivery counts as deduplicated, not as capped, so it is not in the digest (REQ-414).

- Given a subject cooldown is open for (R, s), when a non-test, non-digest delivery for (R, s) is created, then it is Suppressed, and no cooldown is opened or extended.
- Given no subject cooldown for (R, s) and MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown deliveries for R already created Pending in R's open rule cooldown, then it is Suppressed and recorded as suppressed by the cap, for the digest (REQ-414).
- Given both apply, then it is Suppressed as a duplicate, not by the cap (notes).
- Given neither applies, then it is created Pending, a subject cooldown for (R, s) opens for R's cooldown length, and a rule cooldown opens for that length if none is open; an open rule cooldown is not restarted.
- Given a firing for (R, s) every minute with a 10-minute cooldown and the first delivery Sent at once, then the first delivery is Pending, the ones within the next 10 minutes are Suppressed, and the first one after the subject cooldown ends is Pending: a suppressed delivery does not extend the cooldown.
- Concurrency: MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown + 1 deliveries for different subjects of R created before any is sent give exactly MaxDeliveriesPerCooldown Pending ones (notes: deliveries created as pending are counted, not sent ones).
- Given a delivery created Pending that is later Failed, then it still counts towards the cap in its rule cooldown.
- Test and digest deliveries do not go through this rule (REQ-409, REQ-414).

### AC-7 · INV-410 · must

> While a non-test, non-digest delivery created within a subject cooldown or a rule cooldown is pending, the system shall keep that cooldown open.
>
> *Notes:* refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-2 option 1: retries (REQ-412: up to 5 attempts, backoff to 10 min) outlast a typical cooldown, and without this a delivery still retrying is sent alongside the next one. Trade-off accepted: a cooldown can last up to the full retry span, about 20 minutes with the defaults.

- Given a delivery created Pending whose channel keeps rejecting it past the cooldown's length (fake clock), then its subject cooldown and its rule cooldown stay open: a new firing for that subject is Suppressed, and no digest is created while it is Pending.
- Given it is then Sent after the length has passed, then both cooldowns end, and the next firing for that subject creates a Pending delivery.
- Given it is Failed instead, then its subject cooldown closes (REQ-433), and its rule cooldown ends once its length has passed and no other delivery created in it is Pending.
- Property, within the INV-400 and INV-407 generator: no cooldown ends while a non-test, non-digest delivery created in it is Pending.

### AC-8 · REQ-433 · must

> When a non-test, non-digest delivery is marked failed, the system shall close the subject cooldown of its rule and subject, so that the next event for that subject creates a pending delivery.
>
> *Notes:* refiner-model 2026-11-06 F-3 option 1: deduplication assumes the first delivery reached the channel; a failed first delivery must not silence the rest of the cooldown. The rule cooldown (cap) is not closed. Cost: a channel that stays down produces one Failed delivery per event, each logged by REQ-415.

- Given a Pending delivery for (R, s) that is Failed after NotificationMaxAttempts attempts (REQ-415), then the subject cooldown for (R, s) closes, and the next firing for s, with R's cap not reached, creates a Pending delivery.
- Given the same failure, then R's rule cooldown stays open, and the failed delivery still counts towards its cap (notes: the rule cooldown is not closed).
- Given a test delivery or a digest that is Failed, then no cooldown closes.
- Property, within the INV-400 generator: after a failure, no later firing for the same rule and subject is Suppressed as a duplicate of the failed delivery (IntegrationsNotifications.tla's Probe_FailedThenSilent).
- Given R's cap is reached, then the next firing for s after the failure is Suppressed by the cap and listed in the digest (INV-409, REQ-414).

### AC-9 · SCN-400 · scenario

> **Dead-letter alert is sent once per cooldown**
>
> *Given:* Rule R watches dead-lettering of job name sync-crm with a 10-minute cooldown, subject = job name; two sync-crm jobs are dead-lettered 2 minutes apart.

> 2. REQ-407 — sent
